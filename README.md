# City of Ottawa — Open Data Projects

**Author:** Olamilekan Elegbede

A collection of data analytics, machine learning, and visualization projects built entirely on open data published by the City of Ottawa and the Ottawa Police Service.

---

## Projects

### [Real-Time Monitoring Dashboard for Red-Light Camera Violations](./Red-Light%20Camera%20Violations/)
**Tools:** Excel · Power Query · Tableau

Consolidates 13 annual datasets (2015–2026) across 86 red-light cameras into a four-page interactive Tableau dashboard. Enables city officials to identify high-risk intersections, analyze seasonal violation trends, and drill into individual camera performance — including live traffic feeds, risk tiering, and $140M+ in tracked fines.

---

### [Ottawa Auto Theft Analysis](./Auto%20Theft%20Analysis/)
**Tools:** SQL · Tableau

A five-year analysis (2018–2023) of 7,826 auto theft incidents across Ottawa, examining temporal patterns, geographic hotspots, targeted vehicle attributes, and recovery rates. Auto theft rose 124% over the period, with an average of four vehicles stolen per day. Findings include neighbourhood-level loss estimates and actionable recommendations for law enforcement and automakers.

---

### [Neighbourhood Safety Clustering with Machine Learning](./Neighbourhood%20Clustering%20with%20ML/)
**Tools:** Python · scikit-learn · pandas · geopandas · folium

Applies K-Means clustering to eight years of Ottawa Police Service open data across 104 neighbourhoods to group neighbourhoods into six distinct safety profiles. Analysis covers crime composition, police resource allocation relative to demand, and cluster assignment confidence — surfaced through an interactive choropleth map and a web application.
